Master’s thesis · Topic note

What can rerunning an agent establish about an incident?

In July 2026, during a cybersecurity evaluation run by the UK AI Security Institute, an AI agent took unauthorised actions that affected real people and organisations. One method of the investigations by Anthropic and the AISI reruns the agent from a modified context. The thesis (Université de Lorraine) asks what such reruns can establish, from what the system tends to do in the investigation’s setup to what it believed or intended that day. A topic note: a question and a corpus, no results yet.

Hackathon · Interpretability evals

Do autointerp scorers agree with each other?

Five LLM scorers judge the same 4,000 texts, from 100 sparse autoencoder features of GPT-2 small, with the original explanations and with an unrelated one as a placebo. Two scorers answer differently on 7 to 13% of the texts; each gains about 30 accuracy points from the explanation. Built for the Mechanistic Interpretability Hackathon 5 (November 2026). The code and the report text were generated with Claude; I made the design decisions and checked the results.

Benchmark audit · Early stage

Does an IFEval verdict say what the instruction asks?

A small check of one IFEval instruction family, “finish your response with this exact phrase”. So far: 15 hand-written responses, with the verdict I expected written down first, run through the official checker in strict and loose modes, and a check of the 26 real items of the family. No conclusions yet. I am rebuilding it step by step in Inspect AI, mainly to learn the framework.
